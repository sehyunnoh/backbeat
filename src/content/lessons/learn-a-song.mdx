You do not need sheet music to learn a song. You need a method. Here is a simple one that works for beginners.

## Step 1: pick the right song

For your first songs, choose one that is:

- **Slow to medium tempo,** with a clear backbeat.
- **Simple:** a steady beat on the hi-hat or ride, snare on 2 and 4.
- **A song you like,** because you will hear it a lot.

## Step 2: listen and map the song

Use what you learned in the previous lesson. Find the verse, chorus and any bridge, and count the bars.

## Step 3: find the tempo

You can find the BPM of the song with the **tap tempo** on your module: hold **Select** and keep striking a pad in time with the song. The tempo follows how fast you tap. Then you can set the metronome to the same tempo and practise the groove with a click.

## Step 4: simplify

Play the **simplest version** of what the drummer does.

- Use the basic 8-beat if the song is close to it.
- If the hi-hat is busy, play quarter notes instead.
- Skip tricky fills for now and just play the groove.

An easier version played well sounds much better than a hard version played badly.

## Step 5: practise in pieces

1. Play the **verse groove** along with the recording until it is steady.
2. Play the **chorus groove** the same way.
3. Practise the **change** between them: the last bar of the verse and the first bar of the chorus.
4. Add one fill at a time.

## Step 6: slow it down, then play along

If a part is too fast, use a metronome at half speed to practise it, and then raise the tempo. When you are ready, play along with the song itself, through the MIX IN jack of your module.

## Step 7: record yourself

Use the module's **C10 Recording** Coach mode (or your phone) to record yourself, and compare it to the original. You will hear things you did not notice while playing.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Expect it to take several days">
Learning a song is a good use of a week of short sessions. Do not try to do it all in one sitting.
</Callout>

## A mini plan for your first song

| Day | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Listen and map the song |
| 2 | Verse groove with the metronome |
| 3 | Chorus groove with the metronome |
| 4 | The change between verse and chorus |
| 5 | Play along with the real recording |
| 6 | Add one fill, record yourself |
