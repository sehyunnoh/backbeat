**Dynamics** means how loud or soft you play. Two drummers can play the same notes and sound completely different because of dynamics. It is also what makes music feel emotional.

You already know the secret: **stick height controls volume**. This lesson uses that on purpose.

## Three volumes

The chart has three kinds of note on the snare:

- **Small ring:** quiet (low stick)
- **Normal dot:** medium
- **Big dot:** loud (high stick)

Play it with alternating hands. The sticks go to different heights for each volume. Your wrist movement stays the same, only the height changes.

## Make a groove louder and softer

Take the basic 8-beat, and play it four times in a row in a **crescendo** (getting louder):

1. Bar 1: quiet
2. Bar 2: a little louder
3. Bar 3: louder
4. Bar 4: loud, finishing with a crash

Then do the opposite, a **decrescendo**, from loud to quiet.

<Callout kind="note" title="Keep the tempo">
When you play louder, it is tempting to speed up. When you play softer, it is tempting to slow down. Keep the metronome on and watch that your tempo does not change with the volume.
</Callout>

## What to practise

1. **Single hand, three heights:** play eight quiet notes, eight medium, eight loud, in a row.
2. **Groove at three volumes:** the full 8-beat quietly, then normally, then strongly.
3. **Backbeat louder than the rest:** the snare on 2 and 4 should stand out above the hi-hat.
4. **Listen back:** record a minute of playing and check whether the volumes are really different.

## A tip for e-drums

On an electronic kit it is easy to hit every pad at about the same volume without noticing. Your module's Coach mode **C-8 Stroke Balance** shows how hard you hit. Use it to see whether quiet really is quiet.
