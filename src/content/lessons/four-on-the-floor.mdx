**Four on the floor** means the kick drum plays on **every beat**: 1, 2, 3 and 4. It makes a steady, driving pulse and is the foundation of disco, house and a lot of dance and pop music.

## The groove

- **Kick:** every beat (1, 2, 3, 4).
- **Snare:** beats 2 and 4, as usual.
- **Hi-hat:** open on every "&" (the off-beats).

## Closed on the numbers too

For a busier version, play a closed hi-hat on the numbers and open on the &s. Your left foot works the pedal each time:

## Tips

1. **The kick is the metronome.** Play every kick with the same weight and put it exactly on the click.
2. **Open the hi-hat just before the "&".** Lift your left foot, hit the hi-hat on the "&", then press down so it closes on the next beat.
3. **Take it slow.** At 80 BPM it already feels like dancing.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Work on the foot first">
If your right foot gets tired or uneven, practise only the kick on all four beats for one minute while counting. It is the heart of the groove.
</Callout>
