Drummers use a small set of words over and over. Learn these and every video and lesson gets easier to follow. The full list is in the [glossary](../../glossary/).

## The twelve to know

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Tempo / BPM** | How fast the music goes, in beats per minute. 60 BPM is one beat per second. |
| **Beat** | One steady pulse, like a foot tap or one click of the metronome. |
| **Measure (bar)** | A small group of beats. In 4/4 each measure has four beats. |
| **Time signature** | Tells you how many beats are in a measure. 4/4 is the most common one. |
| **Quarter note** | One beat. Counted "1, 2, 3, 4". |
| **Eighth note** | Half a beat. Counted "1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &". |
| **Rest** | Silence that still takes time. Keep counting through it. |
| **Accent** | A note played louder than the others. |
| **Backbeat** | The snare on beats 2 and 4. |
| **Groove** | A repeating drum pattern that makes people move. |
| **Fill** | A short break from the groove, often around the toms. |
| **Rudiment** | A basic sticking pattern, the drummer's alphabet. |

## Why "beat" can be confusing

Drummers use "beat" in two ways:

1. **The beat** is the steady pulse.
2. **A beat** is a drum pattern, like "play me a beat".

You can tell which one from the sentence. In this course we usually say "beat" for the pulse and "groove" for the pattern.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Making it stick">
Pick three words from the table and use each one out loud today, for example: "I am practising at 60 BPM, with an accent on the first note of the measure."
</Callout>
