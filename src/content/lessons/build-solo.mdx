Time to put the tools together. Here is a complete four-bar solo. Each bar uses one idea from this level, and the whole thing has a shape: **start, move, pause, finish**.

## The solo

- **Bar 1: strong start.** A crash and a snare hit together, then steady eighth notes on the snare. Accents on beats 1 and 3.
- **Bar 2: move.** Single strokes down the kit: Snare, Tom 1, Tom 2, Tom 3.
- **Bar 3: pause.** One loud snare hit on 1 and one loud Tom 3 hit on 3, with kicks underneath. Silence in between.
- **Bar 4: finish.** A tom fill: snare, then Tom 1, Tom 1, Tom 2, Tom 3. Land on the crash on the next bar 1.

## Learn it bar by bar

1. **Bar 1:** play it eight times in a row until it is easy.
2. **Bar 2:** the same, using the around-the-drums lesson.
3. **Bar 3:** this is the pause. Count the rests out loud.
4. **Bar 4:** the fill from the tom fill lesson.
5. **Join bars 1 and 2,** then 3 and 4, then all four.

## Make it your own

Once you can play it cleanly, change it. Change **one bar at a time** and keep the other three.

- **Bar 1:** a different rhythm, or start on a tom.
- **Bar 2:** go back up instead of down, or use paradiddles.
- **Bar 3:** use call and response, or a different pause.
- **Bar 4:** a snare-only fill, or a different crash.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Write it down">
Put your own version on paper with the chart notation from the pattern lessons. Writing it down helps you remember it, and next week you can improve it.
</Callout>

## Performing it

- **Play it with the metronome** at 70 BPM first, then try without.
- **Record yourself** using your module's **C10 Recording** Coach mode or a phone. Listen for even notes and clear pauses.
- **If you lose your place, keep the pulse.** Do not stop. Find beat 1 of the next bar and carry on. Soloists do this all the time.
- **Play it for someone.** A friend or family member is a good audience. It teaches you to stay calm.

## What next?

You can now:

- keep a steady pulse,
- play grooves and fills,
- read simple drum music,
- and tell a short story on the kit.

Pick a song you love, and learn it. Or write more solos. Your sticks are ready.
