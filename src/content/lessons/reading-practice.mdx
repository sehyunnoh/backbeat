Time to read real music. For each example:

1. **Look at the staff** first. Find the kick, the snare and the hi-hat.
2. **Say the count** under the staff out loud.
3. **Play it slowly** at 60 BPM, without the chart.
4. **Open the answer** to hear it and check with the chart.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Read one drum at a time">
If a bar looks like too much, read only the kick for one pass, then only the snare, then only the hi-hat. After that, put them together.
</Callout>

## What you just did

You turned dots and lines into drum hits. The more you read, the faster it gets. Try opening any beginner drum book or a free chart online and read just the first four bars.

<Callout kind="note" title="It is a skill, not a talent">
Reading feels slow at first for everyone. A few minutes a day, one groove at a time, makes a big difference within a few weeks.
</Callout>
