The **one drop** is the classic reggae drum pattern. It is named for what the drummer does on beat 1: **nothing**. The "drop" lands later, on **beat 3**, where the **kick and snare play together**. It sounds laid-back and heavy at the same time.

## The groove

- **Hi-hat:** steady eighth notes (or you can play quarter notes for a more open feel).
- **Kick and snare:** together on beat 3. Nothing on 1.

## Tips

- **Beat 1 is empty,** and that is the point. Count "1" out loud even though nothing hits. The silence matters.
- **Kick and snare together:** hit them at exactly the same moment, with equal weight.
- **Relax:** this groove should feel unhurried. Play the hi-hat softly and keep your hands loose.
- **Try quarter notes on the hi-hat** for a sparser sound:

<Callout kind="note" title="Half-time cousin">
The snare on beat 3 is the same idea as the half-time groove from Level 5. The difference is that the one drop also moves the kick to beat 3 and leaves 1 empty.
</Callout>

## Practice

1. Count "1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &" out loud and hit only on 3.
2. Add the hi-hat.
3. Play it for two minutes and keep it relaxed. Everything should feel heavy and slow, even though the tempo is the same as other grooves.
