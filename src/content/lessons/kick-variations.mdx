Once the basic 8-beat is steady, the easiest way to make new beats is to **change only the kick**. The hi-hat and snare stay exactly the same, so your hands never change. Only your right foot does something new.

All three patterns below use the same hi-hat (every eighth note) and snare (2 and 4).

## Kick pattern A: an extra kick after 3

The kick plays on 1, on 3 and on the "&" of 3 (the note right after 3).

## How to learn each one

1. **Foot only first.** Mute the hi-hat and snare (tap their names). Play just the kick and count out loud until it feels natural.
2. **Add the snare.** Kick + snare, still counting.
3. **Add the hi-hat.** Now everything together, slowly.
4. **Speed up** only when it feels easy.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Why this works">
Your hands are on autopilot after the last lesson. That is what you want. Changing only one limb is the easiest way to learn coordination.
</Callout>

## Make up your own

Try moving the kick to other places on the "&"s, while keeping a kick on 1. Some will sound great and some will sound odd. That is the fun part.
