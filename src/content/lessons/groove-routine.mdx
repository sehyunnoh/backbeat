You now have a basic beat, kick changes and an open hi-hat. This routine keeps them sharp and uses the practice tools already inside your TD-1.

## The 10-minute routine

| Step | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 min | Warm up: the one-hand exercises from the Level 1 warm-up lesson. |
| 2 | 2 min | Basic 8-beat at 70 BPM. Use the Metronome button on your module. |
| 3 | 3 min | One of the kick patterns, 60 BPM. Switch pattern every minute. |
| 4 | 2 min | The 8-beat with an open hi-hat on the "&" of 4. |
| 5 | 2 min | Free play: your favourite pattern, at your favourite tempo. |

## Use Coach mode

Press **Select** until the **Coach** lamp is lit, then pick a menu with **[-]** and **[+]**, then press **Metronome** to start.

- **C-4 Auto Up/Down:** the tempo steps up and down by itself, so you practise your beat at different speeds without touching the module.
- **C-5 Quiet Count:** the click goes silent and comes back every four measures. Keep your beat steady while it is quiet. A percentage on the display shows how well you kept time.
- **C-1 Time Check (Easy):** shows if you hit behind, on or ahead of the beat. Use it on your snare backbeat.

## How to know you are improving

- You can play a beat for a full minute without stopping.
- You can tell when your tempo drifts, and pull it back.
- The tempo that feels comfortable goes up, little by little.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Short beats long">
Ten focused minutes every day beat one long session a week. If you only have five minutes, play step 2 and step 3.
</Callout>
