The small box on your stand is the **sound module**. It makes every sound and also has a metronome and a set of practice exercises. Everything on this page comes from Roland's TD-1 Owner's Manual, so it matches your kit.

<Callout kind="note" title="Button names">
On the module some buttons show symbols instead of words. Here they are called **Power**, **Metronome**, **Select**, **[-]** and **[+]**, and **Volume up / down**. If you are ever unsure, check the manual: [TD-1 Owner's Manual (PDF)](https://static.roland.com/assets/media/pdf/TD-1_eng01_W.pdf).
</Callout>

## Power and volume

- Press **Power** to turn on. **Long-press** it to turn off.
- The two **Volume** buttons change the loudness in 20 steps.
- Sound comes out of the **OUTPUT/PHONES** jack. Plug in headphones or a speaker there. Turn the volume down before you plug anything in.
- **Auto Off** switches the kit off after a while of no playing. You can set it to OFF, 10, 30 or 240 minutes. The manual shows how on page 10.

## Choose a drum sound

Your module has **15 preset kits**.

1. Press **Select** until the Drums lamp is lit.
2. Press **[-]** or **[+]** to change kit. The display shows the number.
3. Hit a pad to hear it.

For learning, pick any natural-sounding acoustic kit. Fancy sounds can wait.

## The four modes

**Select** moves between four modes, each with its own lamp:

| Mode | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Drums | Playing, and choosing the kit |
| Tempo | The metronome |
| Coach | Practice exercises |
| Song | 15 built-in songs to play along with |

## The metronome

1. Press **Select** until you reach Tempo mode.
2. Press **Metronome**. The button blinks with the beat. Press it again to stop.
3. Use **[-]** and **[+]** to change the tempo. The range is 40 to 260 BPM.

Handy extras:

- **Tap tempo:** hold **Select** and strike a pad a few times. The tempo follows how fast you hit.
- **Change the click:** in Tempo mode, long-press **Metronome**. Then **Select** chooses what to change and **[-]** / **[+]** change it: number of beats (1 to 9), the note value, the volume and the click sound. Press **Metronome** to finish.
- **Quiet while you play a song:** the manual explains how to switch the click off.

## Coach mode: free practice partners

Coach mode has exercises built for exactly what this course teaches. Press **Select** until Coach is lit, choose a menu with **[-]** / **[+]**, then press **Metronome** to start.

| Menu | Name | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| C-1, C-2 | Time Check (Easy, Hard) | Shows if you hit behind, on or ahead of the beat |
| C-3 | Change-up | The rhythm changes every two measures |
| C-4 | Auto Up/Down | The tempo steps up and down by itself |
| C-5 | Quiet Count | The click goes silent on and off so you keep time on your own |
| C-6, C-7 | Tempo Check (Easy, Hard) | The click gets quieter when you are steady, louder when you drift |
| C-8 | Stroke Balance | Shows how hard you hit, to even out left and right hands |
| C-9 | Timer | Alerts you when your practice time is up |
| C10 | Recording | Records what you play so you can listen back |

Try **C-1 Time Check (Easy)** as soon as you have done the counting lesson. You will use **C-8 Stroke Balance** in the weak-hand lesson later.

## Play along

The **MIX IN** jack lets you plug in a phone or music player so you can play along to songs you like, and you hear both through the same headphones. There are also 15 songs built into the module.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Neighbours">
An electronic kit is quieter than an acoustic one, but the kick pedal and the stick taps still travel through floors and walls. The manual warns about this too. A rug or a mat under the kit helps a lot.
</Callout>
