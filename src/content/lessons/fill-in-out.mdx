Most songs are built from **four-bar phrases**. You play the groove for three bars, then a fill in the fourth, then crash into the next phrase. This lesson puts everything together.

## The loop

- **Bar 1:** crash on 1, then the 8-beat.
- **Bars 2 and 3:** the 8-beat.
- **Bar 4:** groove for two beats, then a snare fill on 3 and 4.

Then it loops: bar 1 starts with the crash again.

## How to practise

1. **Count the bars.** Say "1 2 3 4" every bar and keep track of which bar of four you are in. The fill always lands in bar 4.
2. **Practise bar 4 on its own** until the fill is easy, then put the other bars in front of it.
3. **Loop it:** go around three times in a row, then take a break.
4. **Swap the fill:** try the tom fill from the last lesson in bar 4 instead.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Your best friend: the metronome">
Keep the click going the whole time. If you land on the crash and the click at the same time, your fill was in time. If not, listen to which bar felt rushed.
</Callout>

## What to listen for

- The groove keeps its steady feel in bars 2 and 3 (do not wander).
- The fill sounds like part of the beat, not a surprise.
- The crash lands exactly on beat 1 of bar 1.

When you can do this, you can follow most pop songs on your kit.
