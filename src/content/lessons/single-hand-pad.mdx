Short and regular beats long and rare. Ten minutes a day beats an hour once a week. This warm-up uses everything from Level 1 and takes about six minutes. Do it before every practice session.

## The routine

Use your snare pad, sound on or off as you prefer, and a relaxed matched grip.

| Step | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 min | Shake out your hands and roll your wrists in circles. |
| 2 | 1 min | Right hand, quarter notes at 60 BPM. |
| 3 | 1 min | Left hand, quarter notes at 60 BPM. |
| 4 | 1 min | Right hand, eighth notes at 60 BPM. |
| 5 | 1 min | Left hand, eighth notes at 60 BPM. |
| 6 | 1 min | Rest, then play whatever you like, gently and loosely. |

## While you do it

- Keep the same stick height for every note.
- Breathe. Beginners often hold their breath.
- Check shoulders and wrist tension every minute or so.
- If you lose the beat, stop, count one bar out loud and start again.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Steady tempo practice">
Try Coach menu **C-6 Tempo Check (Easy)** on your module while you do step 2 or 3. The click gets quieter when you keep a steady tempo and louder when you drift, so you hear whether your time is solid.
</Callout>

## Going up

When all steps feel easy for a few days, raise the tempo by 5 BPM. If the sound gets thin or your forearm tightens, go back down. Slow and clean is the goal.
